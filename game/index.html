NEON BAY

A sun-soaked city. A driver with a debt. Five jobs to the top.

Rae Vega owes Mara Quinn — and Mara collects in favours, not cash. Steal cars, lose the NBPD, run the marina exchange, and take down Dario Kestrel, the man who owns the bay's nights. The city is yours: drive it, walk it, work it.

WASD / arrows — move on foot, drive the car  •  E — enter a car, start a job, use the garage

Mouse + click — aim and shoot (pistol, Q to switch)  •  Shift — run  •  Space — handbrake

P — pause  •  M — mute  •  Steal cars, take taxi fares, grab cash, repair at Mara's Garage.

Stars mean the police are coming. Lose them, or pay for a respray at the garage.

Neon Bay is an original game. No part of it copies any commercial game.
