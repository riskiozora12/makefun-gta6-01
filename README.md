Neon Bay — an open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6: a sun-soaked coastal city to explore on foot and by car, traffic and pedestrians, cars to steal and drive, a wanted level with police who respond, five story missions, taxi fares, combat, a HUD with a minimap, and a save that remembers your progress.

Rae Vega owes Mara Quinn — and Mara collects in favours, not cash. Steal cars, lose the NBPD, run the marina exchange, and take down Dario Kestrel, the man who owns the bay's nights.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 — no build step, no network, plain static files under `game/`.

## Play it

- **WASD / arrows** — move on foot, drive the car
- **E** — enter a car, start a job at Mara's Garage, use the garage (repair / respray)
- **Mouse + click** — aim and shoot (pistol; **Q** switches to fists)
- **Shift** — run • **Space** — handbrake • **P** — pause • **M** — mute

Stars mean the police are coming. Lose them out of sight, or pay for a respray at the garage. Your cash, missions and best haul save in the browser.

## What is in the game

- A city of five districts (Old Town, The Docks, Downtown, Marina Bay, South Beach) with a sea, a beach, parks, and named landmarks: Mara's Garage, Bay General hospital, NBPD HQ, the Marina Office.
- Traffic that follows the roads, parked cars to steal, pedestrians who flee gunfire, and police cars and foot cops who chase, ram, and arrest.
- Five story missions with a briefing, objectives, a waypoint beacon, and cash rewards — plus taxi fares and cash bundles in free roam.
- Combat on foot (pistol and fists) and by car (ramming), car damage and smoke, wanted stars that decay when you stay hidden, and a garage that repairs and clears your heat.
- A HUD with health, cash, wanted stars, the current objective, a live minimap, speed, and the district clock; synthesised sound (engine, sirens, gunshots, a bass loop) with no audio files.

Neon Bay is an original game: its city, characters, vehicles, names, story, art and sounds are all its own. No part of it copies any commercial game.
